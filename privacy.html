Privacy at Ube Ube City

Ube Ube City is a game and daily-routine app for young children, made by two families. We built it to collect as little as possible. This page says exactly what that means.

The short version

  • No accounts, no names or emails asked of you, no ads, no tracking, no cookies.
  • What your child does in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The grown-ups' setup is kept in your browser's storage on your device:

  • the child's first name, birthday and grade, if you enter them;
  • school days, bedtime and routines;
  • the weather town;
  • any lines a grown-up records in their own voice.

Your child's progress is kept there too:

  • finished steps;
  • stickers;
  • Ube Ube dollars;
  • the games.

We never receive any of it. To move it to another device, use "Save a copy" in the grown-ups room. That makes a file that only you hold.

On an iPhone or iPad, Safari may clear a website's storage if it is not opened for 7 days. Adding Ube Ube City to your Home Screen keeps it.

The weather

If you turn on the weather, the app asks Open-Meteo for your town's forecast once a day. It sends only the town's map position, never who you are. When you search for a town in setup, the name you type is sent to Open-Meteo to find it.

If you tap "Use my location", your device asks your permission first. The position is used once to name your town, and only the town is kept.

Children

The app is made for children to use with a grown-up nearby. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app has no way for a child to send us anything, chat or buy anything with real money. The settings and the grown-ups room are behind a grown-up question.

The microphone is used only when a grown-up records their own voice for a line in the grown-ups room. The camera is never used.

Deleting everything

In the grown-ups room, "Clear all progress" removes everything the app saved on that device. Clearing your browser's website data does the same.
